About Himanshu Biswal

Himanshu brings over three decades of experience across audit, risk assurance, and financial operations, evolving from a traditional finance leader into a modern, AI-enabled virtual CFO for growing businesses. His philosophy is simple but powerful: finance should not just ensure compliance; it should actively drive growth, clarity, and smarter decision-making.

By combining deep financial expertise with tools like automation, data analytics, and AI, he helps MSMEs transform their numbers into actionable strategies. What he truly loves about his work is enabling business owners to gain confidence in their finances, reduce uncertainty, and unlock opportunities they didn’t see before—turning finance from a back-office function into a strategic growth engine.
